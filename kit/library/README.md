# The research library

WilsonWorks research library, from the Align project, 2026. Deep research reports on health and fitness, copied as
they were written from the private `wilson-works/align` repository (folder `Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/`) on
2026-10-09, on the owner's word: "Check the align repo as well. We probably have a ton of health and fitness research
from there."

Each report cites its own studies (authors, journal and year in the text). A keeper that answers from a report names
the report by its file name and the study the report names for that fact, for example "from the library,
`17_couch_to_5k_evidence_safety.md`, citing Buist et al., 2008".

## Where it sits in the order

When a keeper's cards don't cover a question, it looks in this order: a book Louise already wrote, then this library
(its own folder here), then the web, kept as a card. A report is read and quoted, never copied into a card. Nothing in
it outranks a keeper's law: the emergency lines and the people to see still come first.

## What was left out, and why

- Reports about one person or one place: 01, 05, 06, 12, 13, 14, 16, 24, 28, 32, both 33s, and the OKC gym and pet
  lists. They were written for the Align app and its owner, not for anyone who installs Kindlemere.
- 09, a running and trail plan for one three-year-old cattle dog in Denver: most of it is that city's altitude, trails,
  leash law and weather, so it could not be made general without rewriting it.
- 03 and 08 were not picked for this copy, and the second 31 (the supplement and vitamin database) neither.
- Align's app data (`backend/`: recipes, exercises and the rest) carries no sources, so none of it is here.

Prices in a report (26 names a weekly budget) are the report's, from when it was written. The keepers quote no prices.
