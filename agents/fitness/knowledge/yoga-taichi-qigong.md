# Yoga, tai chi and qigong

What yoga, tai chi and qigong are good for, how strong that evidence is, and how to do them safely.

## Yoga (NCCIH, 2023)

- Low-back pain: slightly better than no exercise; differences may be clinically small. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:169
- Neck pain: less pain and disability. Headaches: fewer and milder, especially tension headaches. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:171 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:172
- Knee osteoarthritis: may improve pain, function and stiffness. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:174
- Anxiety: a substantial beneficial effect. Depression: helps when combined with other treatments. PTSD: only low-quality evidence of possible benefit. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:175 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:176 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:177
- Sleep improvements in cancer patients, people with arthritis and older adults. Balance improved in 11 of 15 studies. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:178 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:180
- Menopause: at least as effective as other exercise. Smoking: 37% more likely to quit during treatment. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:181 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:182
- In the depression network meta-analysis yoga's effect was g -0.55, second to walking or jogging. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:183

## Yoga safety

- Generally safe for healthy people with a qualified instructor. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:188
- Commonest injuries: sprains and strains, mostly knee or lower leg. Serious injuries are rare. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:190
- Emergency-department injury rates are higher in people 65+. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:192
- Pregnancy: avoid hot yoga and long periods lying on the back. If lying back makes you light-headed or unwell, change position. [^49] [^62] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:198
- Beginners avoid extreme poses such as headstands and lotus. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:195
- Modify for prior injuries, spinal disease, hypertension, balance problems and glaucoma. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:196

## Tai chi (NCCIH, 2023)

- Falls in older adults: low-certainty evidence of 19% fewer falls, and high-certainty evidence of 20% fewer people who fall. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:207
- Parkinson's: fewer falls vs no intervention. Stroke: better walking short-term. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:210 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:212
- Osteoarthritis: the American College of Rheumatology strongly recommends tai chi for knee and hip OA; evidence low-to-moderate. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:213
- Fibromyalgia, low-back pain, blood pressure, COPD and cognition: benefits reported. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:216 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:218 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:220 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:222 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:223
- Depression and anxiety: a small effect in older adults with chronic conditions. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:225
- Safety: adverse events were similar to other active or no intervention, and minor. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:227
- Instructors need no licence, and there is no national certification standard. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:230
- Tai chi is the best-supported recommendation for balance and fall prevention in older adults, and it fits WHO's balance advice and ACSM's neuromotor component. [^2] [^4] [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:238

## Qigong and meditative movement

- Qigong in fibromyalgia: 30 to 40 minutes daily for 6 to 8 weeks gave consistent benefits in pain, sleep and function, still present 4 to 6 months later. [^59] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:232
- Tai chi, qigong and yoga gave modest improvements in sleep, fatigue, mood and quality of life, continuing at 3 to 6 months. [^59] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:234
