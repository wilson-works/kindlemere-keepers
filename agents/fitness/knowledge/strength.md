# Strength training

The 2026 and 2009 ACSM resistance numbers, movement patterns, weekly volume, and the health vs muscle-growth dose.

## The 2026 ACSM position stand (asserted)

Label: **asserted.** Louise could not open the 2026 paper itself (paywalled). Every 2026 number below comes from ACSM's own announcement and press coverage of it. Say so when quoting them. [^6] [^7] [^8] [^9]

- ACSM replaced its 2009 resistance-training stand in 2026 (Currier et al., Med Sci Sports Exerc 2026;58(4):851-872). [^6] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:10 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:13
- An umbrella review of 137 systematic reviews, more than 30,000 participants. [^6] [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:18
- Released 16 March 2026, the first major update in 17 years. [^9] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:20
- Outcomes covered include hypertrophy, strength, power, endurance and physical function such as gait speed, balance and stair climbing. [^6] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:21
- Frequency (asserted): train all major muscle groups at least twice a week. [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:28
- Strength (asserted): 80% 1RM or more, 2 to 3 sets per exercise. [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:29
- Hypertrophy (asserted): about 10 sets per muscle group per week. [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:30
- Power (asserted): 30 to 70% 1RM, moved as fast as possible on the lifting phase. [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:31
- Training to failure is optional (asserted). It did not consistently change outcomes for the average healthy adult. [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:35
- Equipment barely matters (asserted). Bands, bodyweight and home routines give marked benefits. [^7] [^9] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:37
- Beginners do not need a different system. [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:39
- The largest gain is the shift from no resistance training to any. Consistency outranks sophistication. [^7] [^8] [^9] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:41
- Individualise on goals, enjoyment and safety, to keep people training. [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:44
- Default for a general adult: all major muscle groups, twice weekly, 2 to 3 sets per exercise, challenging load, stopping short of failure, with whatever equipment the person will actually use. [^6] [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:47

## The 2009 stand: still the granular table

- Novice and intermediate strength: 70 to 85% 1RM, 8 to 12 reps, 1 to 3 sets per exercise. [^5] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:61
- Advanced strength: 70 to 100% 1RM with emphasis on heavy 1 to 6RM, 3 to 5 min rest, 1 to 2 s lifting and lowering. [^5] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:62
- Local muscular endurance: 40 to 60% 1RM, 9 to 15 reps, rest 90 s or less. [^5] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:63
- Power: 0 to 60% 1RM lower body, 30 to 60% upper body, fast, 3 to 5 min rest. [^5] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:64
- Progression rule: add 2 to 10% load once 1 to 2 reps beyond target are done on two consecutive sessions. [^5] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:66
- Where 2009 and 2026 conflict, treat 2026 as current. [^5] [^6] [^7] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:70

## Movement patterns

- The requirement is all major muscle groups on 2+ days a week. [^1] [^2] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:80
- CDC's groups: legs, hips, back, abdomen, chest, shoulders and arms. [^60] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:83
- A six-pattern template covers that list: squat, hinge, horizontal push, horizontal pull, vertical push or pull, core anti-extension. [^60] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:84
- Multijoint exercises are the backbone: 1 to 2 multijoint exercises per major muscle group (NSCA older-adult statement). [^30] [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:87
- The same pattern template runs with bands, bodyweight, dumbbells or machines. [^7] [^9] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:89

## How much: two different questions

- Frequency: twice weekly per muscle group drives hypertrophy and functional strength. [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:96
- Hypertrophy volume: about 10 sets per muscle group per week (asserted, 2026). [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:30
- Health volume: 30 to 60 min a week of muscle strengthening is associated with 10 to 20% lower mortality risk, with no clear extra benefit past about an hour. [^13] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:100
- Ask which one the person means: the small health dose or the larger muscle-growth dose. [^7] [^13] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:106
