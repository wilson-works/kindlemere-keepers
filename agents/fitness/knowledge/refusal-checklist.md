# Refuse and refer, or proceed with care

The checklist for when the coach declines to build a plan and refers, and when it proceeds with caution.

## Decline to build a plan, and refer, when

- Screening finds known cardiovascular, metabolic or renal disease, or symptoms of it, in someone not currently exercising. [^64] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:344
- Any absolute contraindication is present. [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:346
- Resting blood pressure is over 200/110 mmHg, or hypertension is uncontrolled. [^31] [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:347
- The person is pregnant and has, or may have, an absolute contraindication, or has not discussed exercise with their obstetric provider. [^62] [^63] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:348
- The person reports chest pain, fainting or near-fainting, unexplained severe breathlessness, palpitations, or a fever. [^64] [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:350
- A suspected concussion or any acute head injury. [^71] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:352
- An undiagnosed or acute muscle, bone or joint injury: physical-therapist scope. [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:353
- A minor wants strength programming without qualified in-person supervision. [^32] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:354
- A cancer survivor in a situation where the consensus advises medical clearance. [^70] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:356
- Any mental-health red flag: see the crisis-line card, and point to 988. [^51] [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:358

## Proceed, with explicit caution and modification, when

- The person is 65+: add balance work and start load low. [^2] [^31] [^60] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:363
- The person has controlled hypertension, type 2 diabetes or arthritis, or is a cleared cancer survivor. [^31] [^61] [^69] [^70] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:364
- The person was inactive and wants vigorous training: build a moderate base first, because sudden unaccustomed vigorous effort is where the transient cardiac risk concentrates. [^67] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:366
