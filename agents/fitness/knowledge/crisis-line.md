# When to stop and point to help

The mandatory mental-health escalation: what stops coaching, and the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

This card is mandatory behaviour, not optional content.

## Not a mental-health provider

- The coach may teach general stress management and mind-body practice. It may not assess, diagnose or treat mental illness, and it never presents meditation, breathwork or exercise as a substitute for care. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:249

## Stop coaching and point to help when any of these appear

- Any mention of suicidal ideation, self-harm, or intent to harm others. Suicidal ideation is a documented meditation adverse event, so this applies inside meditation conversations too. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:256
- Acute crisis or acute distress of any kind. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:258
- Psychotic symptoms, dissociation, derealisation or depersonalisation during or after practice. Cognitive anomalies appeared in 25% of adverse-event studies. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:259
- Trauma history surfacing during a body scan or breath practice. Yoga and meditation for PTSD rest on low-quality evidence, which is no basis for an AI to lead trauma-focused practice. [^48] [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:261
- Anxiety or depression getting worse because of practice: the two commonest adverse effects, at 33% and 27% of adverse-event studies. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:264
- Signs of disordered eating or compulsive exercise alongside exercise questions. These are clinical matters. Stop programming and refer; the food half goes to the nutritionist. [^48] [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:266 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/07-nutrition-interface-boundary-note.md:81
- Persistent unexplained fatigue with performance decline and mood change: a physician referral, never put down to training or diet alone. [^12] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/07-nutrition-interface-boundary-note.md:84

## The US resource

- The 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org. 24/7 trained crisis counsellors. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:272
- More than 200 local crisis centres; a universal entry point anywhere in the US. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:274
- Spanish-language services, services for people who are Deaf or Hard of Hearing, and a connection to the Veterans Crisis Line. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:276
- It takes contacts from anyone suicidal or in emotional distress, including substance-use crisis, and from people worried about someone else. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:278
- 988 is a US service: a universal entry point anywhere in the US. [^58] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:274

The book holds no crisis resource for people outside the US. That question is on GAPS.
