# Lifting safely, older lifters and young lifters

Technique and blood-pressure cautions, breath-holding, older adults and frailty, youth resistance training, and strength for specific outcomes.

## Technique and blood pressure

- A well-designed programme with instruction in technique and proper spotting is safe for healthy older adults. Instruction and spotting are part of the prescription. [^30] [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:114
- Particular care is owed to the shoulder, knee, hip and spine. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:117
- Uncontrolled hypertension is a contraindication to resistance training. Once blood pressure is controlled, it is generally safe. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:118
- Resting blood pressure over 200/110 mmHg is a relative contraindication to exercise testing. [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:120
- People with unstable medical conditions need a physician's advice before resistance training. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:122
- Power exercises need careful teaching because of the speeds involved. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:124
- Modify intensity and volume for osteoarthritis. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:126
- Breath-holding: do not coach the Valsalva manoeuvre. The safe general cue is to keep breathing through the rep. [^31] [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:128

## Healthy older adults (NSCA 2019)

- 2 to 3 sets per major muscle group, 2 min rest, 2 to 3 days a week. Beginners can start with single sets. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:144
- Reps: beginners 10 to 15; experienced 6 to 12. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:146
- Load: start under 51% 1RM, move to 51 to 69%, and for some, 70 to 85%. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:147
- Headline: 2 to 3 sets of 1 to 2 multijoint exercises per major muscle group at 70 to 85% 1RM, 2 to 3 times a week, plus power work. [^30] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:149
- Power work at 40 to 60% 1RM, explosive on the lifting phase. [^30] [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:151
- Training to failure has not been shown to add anything in older people. 50 to 70% 1RM with excellent form is often a safer, satisfactory stimulus. [^30] [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:153

## Frailty

- Three sessions a week, building to 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:159
- Load from 20 to 30% 1RM, possibly toward 80% over time. Power at 30 to 60% 1RM. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:151 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:160
- Include sit-to-stand and other daily-life movements. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:162
- WHO: multicomponent balance and strength on 3+ days a week for 65+. CDC adds balance activity each week. [^2] [^60] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:166 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:168

## Youth (NSCA 2009)

- Properly designed and supervised youth resistance training is relatively safe. [^32] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:179
- It can improve strength, power, cardiovascular risk profile, motor skills, injury resistance and psychosocial wellbeing, and build habits. [^32] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:181 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:183
- It needs qualified adults who know youth guidelines, plus sensible progression and equipment. [^32] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:186 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:189
- Youth 6 to 17: 60 min a day moderate-to-vigorous including muscle and bone strengthening (WHO: 3+ days). [^1] [^2] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:191
- Affirm that supervised youth strength training is safe and useful. Insist on qualified in-person supervision, never remote programming for minors. [^32] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:194

## Strength for specific outcomes

- Blood pressure: isometric exercise gave the largest resting drop (270 RCTs, 15,827 people): about 8.24 systolic, 4.00 diastolic. Wall squat ranked highest. [^33] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:202 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:205
- That is one network meta-analysis, and isometric holds raise blood pressure while held. No wall squats for someone with uncontrolled hypertension without clinical clearance. [^31] [^33] [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:215
- Depression: strength training Hedges' g -0.49 vs active controls, comparable to CBT (-0.55) in that analysis. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:207
- Type 2 diabetes: ACSM 2022 covers resistance training alongside aerobic work and breaking up sitting. [^69] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/03-strength-and-resistance.md:212
