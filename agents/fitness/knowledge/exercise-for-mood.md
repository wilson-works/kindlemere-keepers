# Movement for mood and stress

Exercise for depressive symptoms and stress, the evidence strength, and planning for the weeks when stress is highest.

## The evidence at a glance

- Exercise for depression: a large network meta-analysis, effects comparable to CBT, but certainty rated low to very low. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:13
- Mindfulness: as good as evidence-based therapies for anxiety and depression, but no long-term effects beyond about 2 months in one analysis. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:14
- Yoga: substantial effect on anxiety. Tai chi: high-certainty 20% fewer people who fall. Breathwork: significant pooled effects on stress, anxiety, depression. Qigong: fibromyalgia benefits with diligent practice. [^49] [^50] [^52] [^59] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:15 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:16 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:17 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:18

## Exercise and depressive symptoms (BMJ 2024)

- 218 studies, 495 arms, 14,170 participants. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:28
- Effect sizes vs active controls (Hedges' g, more negative is better): walking or jogging -0.63; yoga -0.55; strength training -0.49; mixed aerobic -0.43; tai chi or qigong -0.42; aerobic plus psychotherapy -0.54; exercise plus SSRIs -0.55. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:29 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:30 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:31 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:32 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:33 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:34 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:35 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:36
- In the same network: CBT alone -0.55; SSRIs alone -0.26. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:37
- Effects grew with prescribed intensity, and were stronger in groups and with clear prescriptions. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:38
- Certainty: walking or jogging low; every other mode very low. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:40
- The authors recommend exercise alongside psychotherapy and antidepressants as a core treatment. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:42
- So: exercise helps depressive symptoms, with numbers, and the certainty is low. It is never a replacement for care someone already has. [^53] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:45

## Stress

- Exercise first spikes the stress response, then people show lower cortisol and epinephrine after activity. [^55] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:51
- Norepinephrine is implicated: exercise raises it in brain regions that handle stress. [^55] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:53
- Regular exercise can lessen stress effects, improve mood, and help memory and thinking. [^55] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:56
- About five minutes of aerobic exercise can begin to stimulate anti-anxiety effects. [^55] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:58

## What people report (for coaching, not for claims)

- 53% feel good about themselves after exercising; 35% say it puts them in a good mood; 30% feel less stressed. [^54] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:64
- 43% exercise to manage stress, and 62% of those call it very or extremely effective. [^54] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:66
- But 39% of those skipped exercise in the past month when they were stressed. [^54] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:68
- Only 17% of adults exercise daily; 37% less than once a week or not at all. [^54] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:70
- Plan for adherence to drop when stress is highest. Agree a five-minute version in advance, and treat a missed week as normal, not failure. [^54] [^55] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:74
