# Meditation, mindfulness and breathwork

What meditation and breathwork can and cannot do, their documented adverse effects, the real MBSR dose, and the disclosures that go with them.

## What the evidence says (NCCIH)

- Anxiety and depression: mindfulness approaches beat no treatment and worked as well as evidence-based therapies. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:85
- But a 2021 analysis found mixed short-term results and no long-term effects in studies beyond two months. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:87
- Chronic pain: more effective than several treatments, but the studies were low quality. Acute pain: no evidence of reduced severity. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:89 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:91
- Blood pressure: MBSR was linked to a significant drop in people with health conditions. The AHA calls meditation a possible benefit. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:92
- Sleep: mindfulness improved sleep quality more than education and matched approaches like CBT. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:95
- PTSD: six of eight studies reported symptom reduction. Smoking and substance use: cravings fell, but relapse was not prevented better. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:97 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:99
- Use NCCIH's hedged words: "may help", "mixed results", "evidence is not conclusive". [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:101
- Never use meditation or mindfulness to replace conventional care or to put off seeing a provider. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:103

## Adverse effects: the part most fitness content leaves out

- Few studies have examined harms, so firm safety statements are not possible. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:108
- A 2020 review of 83 studies: about 8% of participants had a negative effect, similar to psychological therapies. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:110
- The most common were anxiety and depression. For MBSR, practice was not more harmful than no treatment. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:85 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:113
- Farias et al. 2020 screened 6,742 studies; under 1% defined or measured adverse events. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:115
- Among studies that did, the most common: anxiety (33%), depression (27%), cognitive anomalies (25%), gastrointestinal problems or suicidal ideation (11% each). [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:117
- Adverse events are common during or after meditation and can occur in people with no prior mental-health difficulties. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:120

## The disclosures that go with any meditation or breathwork

- Negative effects are documented at roughly 8% of participants in one review of 83 studies. Comparable to psychological therapies, not zero. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:283
- The most common are anxiety and depression, and they can occur in people with no prior history. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:285
- If practice keeps making you feel worse, stop and see a qualified teacher or clinician. [^48] [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:287
- Meditation and breathwork are never a reason to delay seeing a provider. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:289

## The real MBSR dose

- Standard MBSR: 8 weeks of weekly 2.5-hour classes (up to 3.5), plus an all-day silent retreat of about 7.5 hours in week 6. [^56] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:131
- Home practice up to 45 minutes daily with guided audio. [^57] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:134
- A 2.5-hour orientation and a 5 to 10 minute interview come first. [^56] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:135
- Core practices: awareness of breath, body scan, walking meditation, mindful movement, open awareness. [^57] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:137
- The trial evidence rests on 20+ hours of class and 45 min a day of practice. App micro-sessions do not inherit MBSR's effect sizes. [^48] [^56] [^57] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:140

## Breathwork (meta-analysis of RCTs, 2023)

- Anxiety (20 studies): g -0.32. Depressive symptoms (18 studies): g -0.40. [^52] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:153 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:154
- Conclusion: breathwork may improve stress and mental health. Outcomes were self-reported. [^52] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:152 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:155
- Slow paced breathing with a longer exhale is a reasonable, low-risk tool, with a small-to-moderate effect stated honestly. [^52] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:158
- High-intensity or hyperventilation protocols are outside what this coach leads without supervision. [^51] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/05-mind-body-mental-wellness.md:158
