# FITT-VP, the shape of a plan

The prescription skeleton (frequency, intensity, time, type, volume, progression) and the four training components.

## The skeleton

- ACSM's exercise prescription framework is FITT-VP: Frequency, Intensity, Time, Type, Volume, Progression. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:55
- Frequency: days a week. Intensity: how hard (%HRmax, %HRR, METs, RPE, %1RM). Time: minutes, or sets times reps. Type: aerobic, resistance, flexibility, neuromotor. Volume: MET-min a week, or weekly sets per muscle group. Progression: % load increase, added minutes or sets. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:61 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:63 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:64 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:65
- FITT-VP is applied separately to each of four components: aerobic, resistance, flexibility and neuromotor. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:67
- A prescription is individualised to health status, physical ability, age, training response and the person's goals. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:69
- Intensity anchors: moderate = 3 to 5.9 METs, 64 to 76% HRmax, 45 to 63% VO2max. Vigorous = 6.0+ METs, 77 to 93% HRmax, 64 to 91% VO2max. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:71

## The weekly template (ACSM 2011 position stand)

- Aerobic: 30+ min a day on 5+ days moderate (150+ min a week), or 20+ min a day on 3+ days vigorous (75+ min a week), or a mix. [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:80
- Resistance: each major muscle group on 2 to 3 days a week. [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:82
- Neuromotor (balance, agility, coordination): 2 to 3 days a week. [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:83
- Flexibility: stretches for each major muscle-tendon group, 60 seconds per exercise in total, on 2+ days a week. [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:84
- This stand predates the 2026 resistance-training stand. Where they disagree on resistance specifics, 2026 wins. [^4] [^6] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:86

## A reasoning order for any training question

- Screen first (see the screening card). [^64] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:226
- Set the weekly target from the PAG 2nd edition or WHO numbers for that person's age and situation. [^1] [^2] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:227
- Fill the four components with FITT-VP, separately. [^3] [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:67
- Choose a progression rule before choosing exercises: 2 to 10% load, two consecutive sessions; conservative single-session jumps for running. [^5] [^24] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:231
- Build recovery in by default rather than after symptoms appear. [^12] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:233
- Prefer adherence over optimisation: the largest gain is from doing none to doing some. [^8] [^9] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/01-exercise-science-foundations.md:234
