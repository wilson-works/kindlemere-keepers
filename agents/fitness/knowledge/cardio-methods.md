# Steady, intervals and the fat-burning zone

Aerobic methods (steady-state, HIIT, polarized, tempo, fartlek), what each is good for, and the fat-burning-zone myth.

## The weekly aerobic target

- 150 to 300 min a week moderate, or 75 to 150 min vigorous, or a mix: the adult target in both the US and WHO documents. [^1] [^2] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:10
- ACSM's split: 30+ min on 5+ days moderate, or 20+ min on 3+ days vigorous. [^4] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:12
- Short bouts count. [^1] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:14

## HIIT vs steady (moderate continuous) training

- HIIT has a non-linear dose-response: about 11 min a session reached 80% of the maximal VO2max gain (about 3.45 mL/kg/min). [^20] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:56
- Moderate continuous training was linear and needed about 52 min a session for 80% of its maximal effect. Four to five times longer. [^20] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:59
- In people with cardiovascular disease, HIIT raised VO2peak more than moderate continuous training, more so in programmes longer than 12 weeks, and was safe there. [^21] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:61
- For broader cardiometabolic risk factors, HIIT and moderate continuous training were comparable. [^21] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:64
- The defensible claim is time-efficiency for fitness, not superiority across the board. [^20] [^21] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:66
- HIIT carries higher perceived exertion and higher acute musculoskeletal and cardiac risk, so gate it behind screening and a base of moderate work. [^67] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:66

## Polarized (80/20) training

- Zones in this literature: low below the first ventilatory threshold (under 2 mmol/L), moderate between thresholds (2 to 4 mmol/L), high above the second (over 4 mmol/L). [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:73
- The polarized model is about 70 to 75% low, 0 to 5% moderate, 15 to 20% high, by volume. [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:75
- Review of 14 studies (163 participants in all): 8 of 10 measuring VO2max improved, 5 significantly. Work economy improved significantly in 5 studies. [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:76 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:78
- Limits: small total sample, possible publication bias, short-term only. [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:80
- "Mostly easy, a small slice hard" suits trained endurance athletes. For someone doing 150 min a week it is over-engineering. [^1] [^2] [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:83

## Other named methods

- Tempo or threshold work sits in the moderate band between the ventilatory thresholds, the zone the polarized model minimises. [^23] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:89
- Fartlek is unstructured alternation of faster and easier running. Describe it with the talk test rather than pace. [^15] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:91
- Intervals can be prescribed through FITT: frequency, interval intensity, interval and recovery duration, and mode. [^3] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:93

## The "fat-burning zone"

- Fat oxidation rises with intensity to a peak (FatMax) at roughly 60 to 65% VO2max, then falls steeply. [^27] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:100
- Typical peak rates are 0.3 to 0.6 g/min, with large differences between people. [^27] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:102
- Fat oxidation is minimal at 85% VO2max and above, where carbohydrate dominates. [^27] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:104
- Higher-intensity work burns more total calories, so the fat proportion during a session does not decide fat loss. [^16] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:105
- The zone describes fuel mix, not fat loss. Energy balance, deficits and body-composition targets go to the nutritionist. [^16] [^27] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/02-cardio-and-running.md:109
