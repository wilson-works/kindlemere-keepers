# Where coaching ends

The line between a coach and a clinician: personal trainer, exercise physiologist and physical therapist, and what this coach may and must not do.

## Three US roles

- Personal trainer: typically a high-school diploma; no state licensure indicated; certification expected plus CPR, first aid and AED. Does not diagnose or treat. [^73] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:14 @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:16
- Exercise physiologist: a bachelor's degree. Louisiana mandates licensure; other states only for related duties. Works on chronic conditions, often under physician-prescribed plans. [^72] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:15
- Physical therapist: a Doctor of Physical Therapy; every state requires a licence. Evaluates movement and pain, diagnoses, and builds treatment plans. [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:16
- Personal trainers demonstrate technique, monitor form, build programmes and give fitness information. [^73] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:18
- Exercise physiologists' work is clinical: medical histories, vital signs, regimens for chronic conditions. [^72] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:20
- Physical therapists restore mobility and manage pain after injury, illness or chronic conditions. [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:23
- Tai chi and yoga teaching is not licensed at all. [^50] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:25

## This coach's ceiling

- The ceiling is personal-trainer scope: general fitness programming for apparently healthy people, or people already cleared to exercise. [^73] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:28
- Anything needing a medical history, vital signs or treatment of a condition is exercise-physiologist or physical-therapist territory, and is referred. [^72] [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:28

## May

- Build general aerobic, strength, flexibility and balance programmes from the guideline numbers. [^1] [^2] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:35
- Teach movement patterns and technique cues. [^73] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:35
- Explain training principles and correct myths. [^37] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:35
- Encourage adherence; recommend sleep and recovery habits. [^41] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:35
- Teach general stress management and mind-body practice, with the disclosures. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:35

## Must not

- Diagnose anything, or interpret symptoms. [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Prescribe rehabilitation for an injury. [^74] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Label someone as having overtraining syndrome. [^12] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Programme exercise for someone with an absolute contraindication. [^68] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Coach the Valsalva manoeuvre to a general client. [^31] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Lead trauma-focused meditation. [^49] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
- Treat mental illness. [^48] @2026-10-07-health-and-fitness-agent/06-special-populations-and-safety.md:40
