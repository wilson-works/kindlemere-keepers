# How long food keeps in the fridge and freezer

- Keep the fridge at 40°F or below and the freezer at 0°F or below. [^5] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:5
- Freezer times are for quality only: food kept at 0°F stays safe indefinitely. [^1] [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:6
- Product dates are not a guide to safe use; go by the storage chart. [^2] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:7
- Soups and stews, vegetable or meat added: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 2 to 3 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:8
- Leftover cooked meat or poultry: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 2 to 6 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:9
- Chicken nuggets or patties: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 1 to 3 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:10
- Pizza: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 1 to 2 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:11
- Egg, chicken, ham, tuna and macaroni salads: 3 to 4 days in the fridge; they do not freeze well. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:12
- Casseroles with eggs, after baking: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 2 to 3 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:13
- Quiche with filling, after baking: 3 to 5 days in the fridge, 2 to 3 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:14
- Pumpkin or pecan pie, after baking: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 1 to 2 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:15
- Custard and chiffon pies, after baking: 3 to 4 days in the fridge; do not freeze. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:16
- Hard-cooked eggs: 1 week in the fridge; do not freeze. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:17
- Raw eggs in the shell: 3 to 5 weeks in the fridge; do not freeze them in the shell (beat yolks and whites together, then freeze). [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:18
- Raw hamburger and other ground meat or poultry: 1 to 2 days in the fridge, 3 to 4 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:19
- Fresh chicken or turkey pieces: 1 to 2 days in the fridge, 9 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:20
- Fresh beef, veal, lamb and pork steaks, chops and roasts: 3 to 5 days in the fridge, 4 to 12 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:21
- Bacon: 1 week in the fridge, 1 month in the freezer. Raw sausage: 1 to 2 days in the fridge, 1 to 2 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:22
- Luncheon meat, opened or deli sliced: 3 to 5 days in the fridge, 1 to 2 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:23
- Fatty fish such as salmon and tuna: 1 to 3 days in the fridge, 2 to 3 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:24
- Shrimp and crayfish: 3 to 5 days in the fridge, 6 to 18 months in the freezer. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:25
- Cooked fish: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, 4 to 6 months in the freezer. [^2] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:26
- Gravy and meat broth: 1 to 2 days in the fridge, 2 to 3 months in the freezer. [^2] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:27
- Any other leftovers: 3 to 4 days in the fridge, or 3 to 4 months in the freezer. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/01-cold-storage.md:28
