# Food and medicines, and PKU

## Interactions to screen for (screen and flag; never adjust)
- Warfarin and vitamin K: leafy greens reduce warfarin's effect. The rule is consistency, not avoidance: a steady weekly number of vitamin K servings. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:245
- Changing vitamin K intake moves INR; cranberry juice raises INR. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:246
- MAOIs and tyramine: aged cheese and red wine with MAOIs can cause severe high blood pressure and worse. Linezolid and isoniazid act similarly. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:247
- Grapefruit blocks gut CYP3A4, so more of some drugs enters the blood, and can reduce absorption of others. [^48] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:248
- Affected classes include statins, some calcium-channel blockers, transplant drugs, anti-anxiety drugs, corticosteroids, antiarrhythmics and antihistamines. [^48] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:249
- How strong the effect is varies person to person. [^48] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:250
- Calcium and antibiotics: separate fluoroquinolones by at least 2 hours before or 6 hours after calcium; tetracyclines bind with dairy. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:252
- Potassium with ACE inhibitors or potassium-sparing diuretics: these already raise potassium, so avoid excess potassium. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:253
- Any flagged interaction goes to a pharmacist or prescriber. [^48] [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:256

## PKU (phenylketonuria)
- An inherited disorder of phenylalanine hydroxylase, found by newborn screening. [^65] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:231
- A phenylalanine-restricted diet, started within 10-14 days of birth and kept lifelong, is the core of treatment. [^65] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:232
- Target blood phenylalanine is 120-360 µmol/L for life, with dietitian supervision. [^65] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:234
- Total refusal: PKU is metabolic medicine. Explain what it is and route to the metabolic clinic. [^65] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:237
