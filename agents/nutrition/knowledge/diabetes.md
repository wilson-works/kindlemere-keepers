# Diabetes and prediabetes

- The ADA routes personal medical nutrition therapy to an RDN, preferably one experienced in diabetes (Recommendation 5.10). [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:13
- The ADA endorses no single meal plan and no set macronutrient percentages. It individualises to goals, body and medicines. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:14
- Emphasise non-starchy vegetables, whole fruit, legumes, lean protein, whole grains, nuts, seeds, and low-fat or non-dairy alternatives. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:15
- Minimise red meat, sugary drinks, sweets, refined grains, and processed and ultra-processed food. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:16
- The 2026 Standards suggest considering less carbohydrate for some adults with diabetes, with limiting processed food as an effective route. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:17
- Fiber: at least 14 g per 1,000 kcal. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:18
- Protein: about 0.8 g/kg/day. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:19
- Saturated fat under 10% of calories, about 22 g on 2,000 kcal. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:20
- Sodium under 2,300 mg a day. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:21
- The ADA favours Mediterranean-style and DASH-like patterns. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:22
- Red meat is linked to higher type 2 diabetes risk in a dose-dependent way, which favours plant protein. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:23
- The ADA publishes a Summary of Revisions every year: re-check it each January. [^24] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:24
- The conflict: the DGA's 1.2-1.6 g/kg/day protein sits well above the ADA's 0.8, and may harm people with undiagnosed kidney disease. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:30
- The DGA also allows full-fat dairy (3 servings a day) and butter while keeping the under-10% saturated-fat cap, a practical conflict in diabetes. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:32

## Where the agent stops
- May explain carb counting, glycaemic ideas, fiber targets, label reading and the general shape of Mediterranean and DASH eating. [^5] [^13] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:36
- Must refuse insulin-to-carb ratios, matching carbs to medicine, or a carb gram target for a specific person. That is an RDN's job. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:37
- Never suggest during-exercise carb or electrolyte protocols to a person with diabetes without their clinician. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:191
