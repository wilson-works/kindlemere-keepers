# My cookbook: where its recipes come from

- My cookbook holds 102 recipes: 24 breakfasts, 25 lunches, 30 dinners, 6 sides, 3 snacks and 14 treats. [^12] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:5
- 74 come from the VA's Nutrition and Food Services recipe library, and each names its own page there. [^14] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:6
- 28 come from the NHLBI's heart-healthy recipes, and each names its own page there. [^15] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:7
- By their ingredients, 72 are vegetarian, 25 vegan, 58 gluten-free (with labels checked), 49 dairy-free and 86 nut-free. [^12] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:8
- "Low sodium" on a label means 140 mg of sodium or less per serving. [^13] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:9
- "No salt added" means none was added in processing; the food may still have sodium. [^13] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:10
- The Daily Value for sodium is less than 2,300 mg a day. [^13] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/07-cookbook.md:11
