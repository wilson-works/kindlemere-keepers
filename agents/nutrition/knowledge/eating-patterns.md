# Eating patterns, caffeine and alcohol

## The AHA heart-healthy pattern
- The AHA's 2021 scientific statement supersedes its 2006 diet and lifestyle statement. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:216
- Its ten features: keep a healthy weight; plenty of varied fruit and vegetables; whole grains; mostly plant protein, fish, low-fat dairy, lean unprocessed meat if wanted; liquid plant oils over tropical oils; minimally processed over ultra-processed food; little added sugar; little or no salt; don't start drinking alcohol and limit it if you do; apply it wherever food is made or eaten. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:223

## DASH
- Daily servings at 2,000 kcal: grains 6-8, vegetables 4-5, fruit 4-5, meat/poultry/fish 6 or fewer, low-fat dairy 2-3, fats and oils 2-3. [^13] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:228
- Weekly: 4-5 servings of nuts, seeds, beans and peas; 5 or fewer of sweets. Sodium capped at 2,300 mg a day; 1,500 mg lowers blood pressure further. [^13] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:231
- The DASH-Sodium trial fed 412 adults with untreated raised blood pressure through three 30-day sodium levels. [^14] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:235
- DASH plus low sodium gave systolic pressure 11.5 mm Hg lower than control diet plus high sodium in people with hypertension. [^14] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:236
- On DASH the sodium steps gave smaller drops (1.3 and 1.7 mm Hg) than on the control diet: the two help, but overlap rather than fully stack. [^14] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:238

## Heart disease and blood pressure
- The AHA pattern is the backbone, with DASH plus sodium reduction as the measured intervention. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:74
- Teach the pattern and the sodium arithmetic. Never manage someone on diuretics, potassium-sparing drugs or warfarin without their clinician. [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:86

## Caffeine
- The FDA cites 400 mg a day as not generally linked to harm in healthy adults. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:242
- The FDA estimates toxic effects such as seizures from rapid intake of about 1,200 mg. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:243
- Pure and highly concentrated caffeine products have caused deaths. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:244
- No caffeinated drinks under age 2; medical experts advise against energy drinks for children and teens. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:245

## Alcohol
- AHA: if you don't drink, don't start; if you do, limit it. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:246
