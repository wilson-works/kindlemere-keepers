# Pregnancy and breastfeeding

## Weight gain by pre-pregnancy BMI
- Underweight (under 18.5): 28-40 lb. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:68
- Normal (18.5-24.9): 25-35 lb. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:69
- Overweight (25.0-29.9): 15-25 lb. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:70
- Obese (30 or more): 11-20 lb. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:71

## Energy and nutrients
- Add roughly 350-450 kcal a day. Pregnancy does not double needs. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:75
- Folate rises to 600 µg DFE a day; a prenatal with at least 400 µg folic acid plus folate-rich food is the usual route. [^7] [^50] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:76
- After a prior neural-tube-defect pregnancy, up to 4,000 µg folic acid a day may be needed. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:77
- Iron 27 mg a day, often 30 mg in a prenatal. [^8] [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:78
- Calcium 1,000 mg a day in total; prenatals supply only 200-300 mg. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:79
- Iodine 220 µg in pregnancy, 290 µg when breastfeeding. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:81
- Protein 71 g a day in pregnancy and breastfeeding. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:82
- Omega-3: about 650 mg a day including 300 mg DHA is a common target. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:85
- For supplement questions, the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements fact sheet is the source. [^51] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:86

## Limits and avoidances
- Caffeine: at most 200 mg a day, about two small cups of coffee. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:90
- Alcohol: none. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:91
- Listeria: restrict unpasteurised foods, soft cheeses, deli meats, sprouts and raw fish. [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:92
- Mercury: avoid swordfish, shark, king mackerel, orange roughy, bigeye tuna, marlin, Gulf tilefish. [^52] [^49] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:93
- Do not avoid fish: 8-12 oz a week of lower-mercury seafood in pregnancy and breastfeeding. [^52] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:94
- Best choices include salmon, sardines, anchovies, catfish, tilapia, cod, flounder, shrimp and canned light tuna. [^52] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:95

## Where the agent stops
- Relay published guidance. Refuse to set a weight-gain plan, manage gestational diabetes, or advise restriction in pregnancy; route to the obstetric team. [^49] [^50] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:101
