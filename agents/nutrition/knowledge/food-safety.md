# Food safety

These are the one set of numbers the agent states flatly, with no "consult a professional" wrapper. Hedging on 165°F for poultry is worse than useless. [^68] [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:53

## Safe minimum internal temperatures
- Poultry, all types: 165°F. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:15
- Ground beef, pork, veal, lamb: 160°F. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:16
- Whole cuts of beef, pork, veal, lamb: 145°F, then a 3-minute rest. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:17
- Fish and shellfish: 145°F, or until the flesh is opaque. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:18
- Reheated leftovers: 165°F. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:19
- During the rest the temperature holds or rises, which finishes off the pathogens. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:22
- Only a food thermometer tells you. Colour and texture are not reliable. [^68] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:23

## The danger zone and the two-hour rule
- The danger zone is 40°F-140°F, where bacteria multiply fast. [^69] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:27
- Never leave perishable food out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:28
- Fridge at 40°F or below; freezer at 0°F or below. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:29
- Thaw in the fridge, in cold water or in the microwave, never on the counter. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:31
- Wash hands at least 20 seconds with soap. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:32

## Storage
- Cooked meat or poultry leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge at 40°F. [^71] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:36
- Chicken nuggets or patties and pizza also keep 3 to 4 days. [^71] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:37
- For anything else, point to FoodSafety.gov's Cold Food Storage Chart rather than guess a shelf life. [^71] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:39
- FSIS has separate pages for freezing and for refrigeration, for quality-versus-safety questions. [^79] [^80] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:40

## The four steps
- Clean, Separate, Cook, Chill. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:44
- Clean: hands 20 seconds, and surfaces. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:46
- Separate: raw food apart from ready-to-eat food. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:47
- Cook: to the temperatures above. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:48
- Chill: refrigerate promptly, inside the 2-hour window. [^67] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:49
