# Portioning a plate or a prep box

- Start with a 9-inch plate. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:5
- Fill half the plate with non-starchy vegetables such as salad, green beans or broccoli. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:6
- Fill a quarter with lean protein such as chicken, beans, tofu or eggs. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:7
- Fill a quarter with carbohydrate foods: grains, starchy vegetables, rice, pasta, beans, fruit or yogurt. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:8
- Drink water or a low-calorie drink with the meal. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:9
- Eating about the same amount of carbohydrate at each meal can help steady blood sugar; the amount is for a doctor or dietitian to set. [^7] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:10
- The 2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines (the New Pyramid) suggest 3 servings of vegetables and 2 of fruit a day. [^8] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:11
- They suggest 2 to 4 servings of whole grains a day. [^8] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:12
- The New Pyramid is a flexible framework for choices, not a set menu. [^8] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:13
- Hand portions: a palm of protein (about 4 oz cooked meat), a fist of vegetables (about 1 cup), a cupped hand of carbohydrate (about 1/2 to 2/3 cup cooked grains, or a medium fruit), a thumb of fat (about 1 tablespoon). [^11] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:14
- A starting point is one of each hand portion per meal for women and two for men, changed one portion at a time. [^11] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/05-plate-portions.md:15
