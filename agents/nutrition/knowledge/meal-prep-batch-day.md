# A batch-prep day for the week

- Portion a big batch into shallow containers before chilling it, so it cools quickly. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:5
- Label frozen portions with what is inside and the date. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:6
- Where a report gives a longer fridge time than the federal chart, the chart wins: soups, stews and egg casseroles keep 3 to 4 days. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:7
