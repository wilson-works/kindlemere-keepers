# A batch-prep day for the week

- Start whatever cooks longest first, and run the oven, the stovetop and the slow cooker at the same time. [^9] [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:5
- The evening before: clear two fridge shelves, set out clean containers, and move frozen meat to the fridge to thaw. [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:6
- One 2 to 2.5 hour session can cover the week: a sheet-pan protein in the oven, a pot of grains and a pot of chili or soup on the stove, then egg cups once the oven is free. [^9] [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:7
- While those cook, chop the week's vegetables and portion out frozen vegetables. [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:8
- Spread cooked rice on a sheet pan so it cools quickly, or portion it straight into containers. [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:9
- Portion into single meals before chilling; shallow containers cool fastest. [^10] [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:10
- Eat the fridge meals first, through day 4, and freeze the portions for days 5 to 7. [^9] [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:11
- Move the next frozen portions to the fridge the evening before; they thaw in 24 to 36 hours. [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:12
- Label frozen portions with what is inside and the date. [^10] [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:13
- Freeze burrito bowls without the salsa, and add it fresh when you eat. [^10] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:14
- Rotate sauces and toppings so one batch tastes different across the week. [^9] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:15
- Where a report gives a longer fridge time than the federal chart, the chart wins: soups, stews and egg casseroles keep 3 to 4 days. [^1] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:16
- Vegetarian swap for a batch chili: tofu crumbles and an extra can of beans in place of the ground meat. [^9] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:17
- Dairy-free swap: unsweetened soy milk for milk, and crumbled firm tofu for cottage cheese. [^9] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:18
- Gluten-free swap: rice, potatoes, corn tortillas and tamari in place of wheat bread, pasta and soy sauce. [^9] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/04-batch-prep.md:19
