# Adapting a recipe

The book teaches how to adapt recipes. It holds no recipes of its own: that is on the gaps list for Louise.

## Toward nutrition targets
- Nutrition.gov's framing: modify recipes to reduce fat, calories, sodium and sugar while raising fiber. [^72] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:133
- Targets: saturated fat under 10% of calories, added sugars under 10%, sodium under 2,300 mg a day. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:139
- Fiber at least 14 g per 1,000 kcal, or the 28 g label Daily Value. [^5] [^10] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:140
- Liquid plant oils in place of tropical oils and partially hydrogenated fats. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:141
- Prepare food with little or no salt. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:142
See the food-allergies card for the allergy and intolerance swap procedure.

## Inside a therapeutic pattern
- DASH: rebuild the week to the DASH servings at 2,000 kcal. [^13] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:160
- Diabetes: shift toward non-starchy vegetables, whole fruit, legumes, lean protein, whole grains, nuts, seeds and low-fat dairy, away from red meat, sugary drinks, sweets and refined or ultra-processed food. [^23] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:161
- Low-FODMAP: adapt only inside a dietitian-supervised 2-6 week phase 1. Never write permanent low-FODMAP recipes. [^36] [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:162
- Renal: do not adapt. Kidney prescriptions are clinician-set. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:163
- Vegan: adapt freely on macros, but carry the B12, iodine, iron, choline, vitamin D and calcium checklist into the plan. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:164

## Why cook from whole food
- The ultra-processed arm of the NIH trial ate 508 ± 106 kcal a day more at matched macros. [^15] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:173
- The AHA lists choosing minimally processed food as one of its ten features. [^12] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:177
- The 2025-2030 DGA's core message is whole, nutrient-dense food and less highly processed food. [^1] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:179
