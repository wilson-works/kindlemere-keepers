# Cooling, reheating and thawing a batch safely

- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour when it is above 90°F. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:5
- Throw away perishable food left at room temperature longer than that. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:6
- Bacteria grow fast between 40°F and 140°F; keep hot food at 140°F or warmer. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:7
- Cool a batch fast by splitting it into shallow containers: a big pot of soup cools too slowly. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:8
- Cut large items such as roasts into smaller pieces before chilling them. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:9
- Hot food can go straight into the fridge, or into an ice or cold-water bath first. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:10
- Cover, wrap airtight or seal leftovers in containers, then refrigerate or freeze them right away. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:11
- Reheat leftovers to 165°F on a food thermometer. [^3] [^5] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:12
- Bring sauces, soups and gravies to a rolling boil when reheating them. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:13
- In the microwave, cover and vent the dish, stir or rotate it, and check the temperature in several places: microwaves leave cold spots. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:14
- Frozen leftovers can be reheated without thawing: soups and stews in a pan or microwave, casseroles in the oven or microwave. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:15
- Cooking or reheating from frozen takes about one and a half times as long. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:16
- Thaw food in the fridge, in cold water or in the microwave; never on the counter. [^4] [^5] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:17
- Fridge thawing is slowest but safe the whole time: small items thaw overnight, most foods take a day or two. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:18
- Cold-water thawing: seal the food in a leak-proof bag, change the water every 30 minutes, and cook it right after. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:19
- Cook food thawed in the microwave right away. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:20
- Leftovers thawed in the fridge keep 3 to 4 more days, or can be refrozen. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:21
- Food thawed in cold water or the microwave is cooked before it is refrozen. [^3] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:22
- Freeze leftovers within 3 to 4 days. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:23
- Never refreeze food that sat out of the fridge longer than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F). [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:24
- Wash hands at least 20 seconds with soap, and use one board for raw meat and another for produce. [^5] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/02-cool-reheat-thaw.md:25
