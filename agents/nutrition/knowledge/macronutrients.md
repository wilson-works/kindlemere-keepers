# Macronutrients and water

## AMDR: share of total energy
- Ages 1-3: carbohydrate 45-65%, protein 5-20%, fat 30-40%. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:69
- Ages 4-18: carbohydrate 45-65%, protein 10-30%, fat 25-35%. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:70
- Ages 19+: carbohydrate 45-65%, protein 10-35%, fat 20-35%. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:71

## RDA or AI per day (carb g, protein g, fiber g, total water)
- Ages 1-3: carb 130, protein 13, fiber 19, water 1.3 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:81
- Ages 4-8: carb 130, protein 19, fiber 25, water 1.7 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:82
- Ages 9-13: carb 130, protein 34, fiber 26-31, water 2.1-2.4 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:83
- Males 14-18: carb 130, protein 52, fiber 38, water 3.3 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:84
- Males 19+: carb 130, protein 56, fiber 30-38, water 3.7 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:85
- Females 14-18: carb 130, protein 46, fiber 26, water 2.3 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:86
- Females 19-50: carb 130, protein 46, fiber 25, water 2.7 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:87
- Females 51+: carb 130, protein 46, fiber 21, water 2.7 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:88
- Pregnancy: carb 175, protein 71, fiber 28, water 3.0 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:89
- Lactation: carb 210, protein 71, fiber 29, water 3.8 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:90
- Total water counts all beverages plus the water in food. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:75

## Notes that travel with these numbers
- The adult protein RDA is 0.8 g/kg/day; 56 g and 46 g are reference-body-weight conversions of it. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:94
- There is no RDA or AI for total fat in anyone over 12 months, only the AMDR. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:95
- The 130 g/day carbohydrate RDA supplies the brain's glucose need. It is not a cap. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:96
- An alternative fiber target in wide clinical use is at least 14 g per 1,000 kcal, which the ADA applies. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:97
- Label Daily Values: fiber 28 g, added sugars 50 g, saturated fat 20 g, sodium 2,300 mg. [^10] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:98
