# Protein: never one number

## The rule
- Never give a single protein number. Branch on kidney function known or unknown, age, and training load, and say which branch you took. Kidney function is the branch that harms if skipped. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:111
- Proactively flag high-protein advice as unsafe when kidney function is unknown, because most CKD is undiagnosed. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:68

## The branches
- Baseline RDA for adults: 0.8 g/kg/day. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:102
- 2025-2030 DGA, general population: 1.2-1.6 g/kg/day (contested; see the frameworks card). [^2] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:103
- ADA, people with diabetes: about 0.8 g/kg/day, with no evidence that sustained higher intake adds cardiometabolic benefit. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:104
- Exercising adults: 1.4-2.0 g/kg/day. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:105
- Healthy older adults: at least 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day. [^57] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:106
- CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis: 0.55-0.60 g/kg/day, set by a clinician. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/01-overview.md:107

## Older adults
- PROT-AGE and ESPEN recommend at least 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day for healthy older adults. [^57] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:117
- Up to 1.2-1.5 g/kg/day is indicated in older people with acute or chronic disease. [^57] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:118
- For malnourished older adults the book cites up to 2.0 g/kg/day. [^58] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:119
- Shortfall is common: in UK adults 65-89, 36% missed 0.75 g/kg/day and 85% missed 1.2 g/kg/day. [^66] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:122
- Older adults may need up to 40 g protein per serving to maximise muscle protein synthesis, against about 20 g in younger adults. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:123
- Quote 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day for a healthy adult over 65, but know kidney function first: the CKD prescription runs the other way (0.55-0.60 g/kg/day). [^57] [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:127

## When training (the nutrition side only)
- Joint ACSM, AND and DC position for athletes: 1.2-2.0 g/kg/day. [^81] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:38
- ISSN, building or keeping muscle: 1.4-2.0 g/kg/day. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:39
- ISSN, energy restriction with resistance training: 2.3-3.1 g/kg/day to keep lean mass, for healthy exercising people only; never with known or suspected kidney disease, where the clinician-set figure is 0.55-0.60 g/kg/day. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:40
- ISSN, resistance-trained and cutting hard: above 3.0 g/kg/day "may promote additional fat-mass reduction", for healthy exercising people only; never with kidney disease (0.55-0.60 g/kg/day there). [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:41
- The ISSN reports no adverse effects from higher intakes in healthy, exercising people at the recommended levels. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:44
- That safety statement is scoped to healthy people and does not carry over to kidney disease. [^83] [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:45

## Per meal
- About 0.25 g of high-quality protein per kg body weight per serving, or 20-40 g absolute. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:49
- Each serving with 700-3,000 mg leucine plus a balanced array of essential amino acids. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:51
- Spread doses every 3-4 hours across the day. [^83] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:52
- Early recovery (0-2 hours after exercise): 0.25-0.3 g/kg, or 15-25 g. [^81] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:53
- 30-40 g casein about 30 minutes before sleep raised overnight muscle protein synthesis. [^84] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:54

## The "anabolic window" myth
- Muscles stay sensitised to protein for at least 24 hours after a resistance session. [^84] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:58
- Pre- and post-exercise protein both stimulate muscle protein synthesis, so timing is flexible. [^84] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:59
- Asked about a 30-minute window: total daily intake and 3-4 hourly spread matter most; the sensitised period runs about 24 hours. [^83] [^84] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:64
