# IBS, GERD and PCOS

## IBS and the low-FODMAP diet
- The ACG's 2021 IBS guideline recommends a limited trial of low-FODMAP for global symptoms. [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:127
- That is a conditional recommendation on very low quality evidence. [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:128
- Its complexity and deficiency risk need a properly trained GI dietitian. [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:129
- Even so, it is the most evidence-based dietary approach for IBS. [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:130
- Phase 1, low FODMAP: restrict high-FODMAP foods under a dietitian, for 2-6 weeks. [^36] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:137
- Phase 2, reintroduction: retest FODMAP groups one by one, about 6-8 weeks. [^36] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:138
- Phase 3, personalisation: long term, restricting only the groups the person reacts to. [^36] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:139
- Phase 1 is not a diet for life. The restriction exists to learn, then is relaxed. [^36] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:142
- The agent may explain the phases and say plainly that phase 1 should not run indefinitely. It must not design or supervise one. [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:147
- Never write permanent low-FODMAP recipes. [^36] [^35] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:162

## GERD (reflux)
- Weight loss in overweight people helps symptoms: moderate evidence, strong recommendation. [^38] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:153
- No meals within 2-3 hours of bed: low evidence, conditional recommendation. [^38] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:154
- Avoiding individual "trigger foods": low evidence, conditional recommendation. [^38] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:155
- So never recite "avoid coffee, chocolate, tomatoes" as fact. Say the evidence is low and conditional. [^38] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:160

## PCOS
- The 2023 international guideline finds no evidence that any one diet composition beats another, for any outcome. [^39] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:166
- Lifestyle change (exercise, or diet plus exercise plus behaviour) is recommended for everyone with PCOS. [^39] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:168
- Healthy eating and activity are recommended for all women with PCOS. [^40] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:169
- A healthy lifestyle helps even without weight loss. [^40] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:170
- This is where internet claims (keto, "insulin-resistance diets", inositol protocols) get parroted. The guideline's finding: sticking with it matters more than the composition. [^39] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:174
