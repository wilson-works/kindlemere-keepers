# A batch-prep day for the week

Researched for Avo's kitchen, 2026-10-09 (lane AVO, n1009). One fact per line, in plain words, with its source and address.

- Start whatever cooks longest first, and run the oven, the stovetop and the slow cooker at the same time. [^9] [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md ; https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- The evening before: clear two fridge shelves, set out clean containers, and move frozen meat to the fridge to thaw. [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- One 2 to 2.5 hour session can cover the week: a sheet-pan protein in the oven, a pot of grains and a pot of chili or soup on the stove, then egg cups once the oven is free. [^9] [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md ; https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- While those cook, chop the week's vegetables and portion out frozen vegetables. [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- Spread cooked rice on a sheet pan so it cools quickly, or portion it straight into containers. [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- Portion into single meals before chilling; shallow containers cool fastest. [^10] [^3] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md ; https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/leftovers-and-food-safety)
- Eat the fridge meals first, through day 4, and freeze the portions for days 5 to 7. [^9] [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md ; https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- Move the next frozen portions to the fridge the evening before; they thaw in 24 to 36 hours. [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- Label frozen portions with what is inside and the date. [^10] [^6] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md ; https://ohioline.osu.edu/factsheet/HYG-5308)
- Freeze burrito bowls without the salsa, and add it fresh when you eat. [^10] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/08_budget_high_protein_diet_60_week.md)
- Rotate sauces and toppings so one batch tastes different across the week. [^9] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md)
- Where a report gives a longer fridge time than the federal chart, the chart wins: soups, stews and egg casseroles keep 3 to 4 days. [^1] (https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/cold-food-storage-charts)
- Vegetarian swap for a batch chili: tofu crumbles and an extra can of beans in place of the ground meat. [^9] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md)
- Dairy-free swap: unsweetened soy milk for milk, and crumbled firm tofu for cottage cheese. [^9] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md)
- Gluten-free swap: rice, potatoes, corn tortillas and tamari in place of wheat bread, pasta and soy sauce. [^9] (https://github.com/wilson-works/align/blob/main/Gym_DEEP_Research_Reports/26_budget_meal_prep_high_protein.md)
