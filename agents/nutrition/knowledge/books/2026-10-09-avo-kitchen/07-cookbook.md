# My cookbook: where its recipes come from

Researched for Avo's kitchen, 2026-10-09 (lane AVO, n1009). One fact per line, in plain words, with its source and address.

- My cookbook holds 102 recipes: 24 breakfasts, 25 lunches, 30 dinners, 6 sides, 3 snacks and 14 treats. [^12] (https://www.nutrition.va.gov/NUTRITION/Recipes.asp)
- 74 come from the VA's Nutrition and Food Services recipe library, and each names its own page there. [^14] (https://www.nutrition.va.gov/NUTRITION/Recipes.asp)
- 28 come from the NHLBI's heart-healthy recipes, and each names its own page there. [^15] (https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/healthy-foods/healthy-eating-recipes)
- By their ingredients, 72 are vegetarian, 25 vegan, 58 gluten-free (with labels checked), 49 dairy-free and 86 nut-free. [^12] (https://www.nutrition.va.gov/NUTRITION/Recipes.asp)
- "Low sodium" on a label means 140 mg of sodium or less per serving. [^13] (https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-education-resources-materials/sodium-your-diet)
- "No salt added" means none was added in processing; the food may still have sodium. [^13] (https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-education-resources-materials/sodium-your-diet)
- The Daily Value for sodium is less than 2,300 mg a day. [^13] (https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-education-resources-materials/sodium-your-diet)
