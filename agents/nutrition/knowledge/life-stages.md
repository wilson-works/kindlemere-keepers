# Life stages

## Fill these before any number
- Several values move 2x or more across life stages: iron 8 to 27 mg, folate 400 to 600 µg, protein 46 to 71 g. So "who is asking" comes first. [^6] [^7] [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:13
- Age band (infant, child, adolescent, adult, 51+, 71+). [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:17
- Sex, and pregnancy or breastfeeding. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:18
- Activity load, which changes energy and sometimes protein and fluid. [^81] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:19
- Known medical conditions and medicines. [^23] [^47] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:20

## Infants (0-12 months)
- The AAP recommends exclusive breastfeeding for about 6 months. [^55] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:26
- All breastfed infants should get at least 400 IU vitamin D a day from the first few days. [^56] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:27
- No honey in any form in the first year: infant botulism. [^54] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:29
- No cow's milk as a drink before 12 months. [^54] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:30
- No added salt or sugar in homemade baby food; avoid canned foods high in both. [^54] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:31
- Peanut foods from about 4-6 months; test first only infants with moderate-to-severe eczema or egg allergy. [^45] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:34
- From age 1, 2 servings a week of lower-mercury fish, 1 oz servings at ages 1-3. [^52] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:35
- No caffeinated drinks under 2. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:36
- Relay the published rules only. Faltering growth, reflux, suspected milk-protein allergy or prematurity go to the paediatrician. [^55] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:40

## Children and teens
- Fat AMDR is wider in young children: 30-40% of energy at 1-3 years. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:46
- Fiber AI climbs: 19 g (1-3), 25 g (4-8), 26-31 g (9-13), 38 g boys 14-18, 26 g girls 14-18. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:49
- Protein RDA: 13 g (1-3), 19 g (4-8), 34 g (9-13), 52 g boys 14-18, 46 g girls 14-18. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:50
- WHO fiber for children: 15 g (2-5) and 21 g (6-9). [^11] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:52
- Fish serving sizes scale with age: 1 oz (1-3), 2 oz (4-7), 3 oz (8-10), 4 oz (11). [^52] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:53
- Energy drinks are advised against for children and teens. [^17] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:54
- The 2025-2030 DGA advises avoiding added sugars entirely, especially for children. [^2] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:55
- Iron needs split at menarche, and boys' and girls' protein and fiber values separate at 14. [^6] [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:58

## Older adults (51+, 71+)
- Calcium rises to 1,200 mg (women 51+, men 71+), and its UL drops to 2,000 mg at 51+. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:109
- Vitamin D rises to 800 IU above 70. [^7] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:110
- Women's iron falls from 18 to 8 mg after menopause. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:112
- Fiber AI falls with energy needs: 30 g men 51+, 21 g women 51+. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:113
See the protein card for protein in older adults.

## Men and women, ages 19-50
- Iron: men 8 mg, women 18 mg before menopause. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:137
- Zinc: men 11 mg, women 8 mg. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:138
- Magnesium: men 400-420 mg, women 310-320 mg. [^90] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:146
- Potassium: men 3,400 mg, women 2,600 mg. [^8] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:147
- Fiber: men 38 g, women 25 g. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:148
- Total water: men 3.7 L, women 2.7 L. [^6] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:149
- Mifflin-St Jeor differs by sex only in its constant: +5 for men, −161 for women. [^9] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:152
