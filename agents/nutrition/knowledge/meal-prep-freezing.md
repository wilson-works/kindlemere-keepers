# What freezes well, what does not, and how to pack it

- Almost any food can be frozen; canned food and eggs in the shell are the exceptions. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:5
- Some foods freeze safely but come back poorly, such as mayonnaise, cream sauce and lettuce. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:6
- Cooked egg white turns tough, soft or spongy in the freezer. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:7
- Cooked pasta loses its texture when frozen. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:8
- Mature potatoes turn grainy, watery or dark when frozen. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:9
- Fried foods turn soggy and taste stale after freezing. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:10
- Mayonnaise, sour cream and cream sauces separate in the freezer. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:11
- Raw apples and grapes turn mushy when frozen. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:12
- Salad vegetables (cabbage, celery, cucumbers, lettuce, tomatoes) go limp and water-logged. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:13
- Cheese and crumb toppings go soggy: add them when you reheat. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:14
- Pepper, cloves and garlic can turn strong in the freezer, so season lightly and adjust when serving. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:15
- Raw meat and poultry keep their quality frozen longer than cooked, because cooking takes out moisture. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:16
- For best quality: casseroles 2 to 3 months, soups and stews 2 to 3 months, cooked meat 2 to 3 months, cooked poultry 4 months, frozen dinners 3 to 4 months. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:17
- Combination main dishes are best used within three months. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:18
- Pack food in the serving sizes you will use. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:19
- Label each package with what it is and the date. [^6] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:20
- Freeze fast: lay new packages in one layer and stack them only once they are frozen solid. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:21
- A food 2 inches thick should freeze through in about 2 hours. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:22
- Freezer burn (grayish-brown dry spots) hurts quality, not safety: cut it away. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:23
- Freezing does not destroy nutrients. [^4] @2026-10-09-avo-kitchen/03-freezing.md:24
