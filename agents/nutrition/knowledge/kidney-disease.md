# Chronic kidney disease (CKD) and dialysis

This is the hardest refusal in the knowledge. The agent explains; a renal dietitian prescribes.

- The reference is the KDOQI 2020 nutrition-in-CKD guideline. [^26] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:43
- CKD 3-5, stable, not on dialysis: 0.55-0.60 g/kg/day (a low-protein diet). [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:47
- CKD 3-5, very-low-protein route: 0.28-0.43 g/kg/day plus keto-acid or amino-acid analogues. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:48
- CKD 3-5 with diabetes: 0.6-0.8 g/kg/day per KDOQI; KDIGO states 0.8 g/kg/day. [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:49
- CKD 5D on haemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis, stable: 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:50
- KDIGO recommends under 2 g sodium a day for diabetes and CKD. [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:54
- KDOQI's guideline uses 2.3 g a day, and the groups judged the gap not clinically meaningful. [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:55
- KDOQI favours vegetable protein sources, on observational evidence rather than trials. [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:56

## How strong the evidence is
- A 2009 Cochrane review found protein restriction "may slow progression to kidney failure, but to a nonsignificant degree". [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:60
- Later meta-analyses showed more benefit in type 1 than type 2 diabetes. [^27] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:61

## Where the agent stops
- Must refuse to set a protein, potassium, phosphorus or sodium prescription for anyone with CKD or on dialysis. It is managed like a medicine and needs a renal dietitian. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:66
- May explain the guideline ranges and why they differ on dialysis, and recommend a renal RDN. [^25] [^26] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:67
- Must flag high-protein advice as unsafe when kidney function is unknown, because most CKD is undiagnosed. [^5] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/02-medical-diets-allergies.md:68
- Do not adapt recipes for a renal diet: those prescriptions are clinician-set. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:163
- Sports protein ranges do not apply to anyone with CKD. [^25] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/05-nutrition-exercise-interface.md:172
