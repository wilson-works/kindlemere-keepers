# Plant-based eating, and genes

## Vegetarian and vegan
- The Academy's position "Vegetarian Dietary Patterns for Adults" was approved January 2025 and runs to 31 December 2032. [^59] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:170
- Well-planned vegetarian and vegan eating can be nutritionally adequate and can improve several heart and metabolic outcomes. [^59] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:171
- Nutrients of concern: vitamin B12, iodine, iron, choline and vitamin D, plus calcium for vegans. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:172
- B12 deficiency among healthy adult vegetarians ranged 30-86%, and among vegans 43-88%. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:173
- Vegetarian and vegan eating may be linked to lower ferritin, with much iron deficiency in vegetarian premenopausal women. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:174
- Run the fixed checklist for any plant-based person (B12, iodine, iron, choline, vitamin D, plus calcium if vegan), and treat B12 as supplement-or-fortified-food-mandatory. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:179
- When adapting vegan meals, carry that checklist into the plan. [^60] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/04-cooking-meal-prep-budget.md:164

## DNA diets: the evidence
- A scoping review found 104 companies selling 204 nutrition-gene panels on limited evidence. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:186
- Food4Me, a 6-month RCT in seven European countries, had four arms: generic advice; personalised from diet; diet plus body measures; diet plus body measures plus genes. [^61] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:190
- Result: "no evidence that including phenotypic and phenotypic plus genotypic information enhanced the effectiveness". [^61] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:191
- Personalised advice beat generic advice. The gain came from personalising, not from DNA. [^61] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:193
- The most-tested genes were FTO, HLA-DQ, MTHFR, PPARG and TCF7L2. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:202
- FTO, PPARG and TCF7L2 links to weight show only modest effects, with no significant effects in meta-analyses. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:205
- Most polygenic advice lacks trial validation, genes do not act in isolation, and company panels are mostly not peer reviewed. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:206

## Where gene and diet really meet
- Lactase persistence: an MCM6 regulatory element controls LCT, and variants there keep lactase on for life. [^63] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:213
- Adult lactose intolerance is the normal decline of LCT after infancy in most people. [^64] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:215
- Even so, the practical answer is finding the dose: many tolerate about 12 g lactose. [^46] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:217
- PKU: one gene, lifelong phenylalanine restriction, blood target 120-360 µmol/L. [^65] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:218
- HLA-DQ testing is among the better-established uses, but carrying it is not a celiac diagnosis. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:219
- MTHFR's link to folate is real; the leap to a personal supplement protocol is not evidenced. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:220

## The rules on genetics
- Never base a nutrient prescription on a consumer DNA report. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:224
- Asked if DNA testing improves diet outcomes: cite Food4Me's null result for the gene arm. [^61] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:225
- Three tiers: one-gene diet disease (PKU, real, clinical); a single well-known variant with modest effect (lactase, managed by dose); polygenic panels (FTO, PPARG, TCF7L2, not actionable). [^65] [^63] [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:226
- Say "genes do not act in isolation", never that a variant decides a diet. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:227
- Route any genetic result with clinical meaning to a clinician or genetic counsellor. [^62] @2026-10-07-nutritionist-agent/03-life-stage-demographics.md:228
