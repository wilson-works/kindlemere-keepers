# How long, how often, and when to raise the bar

## Session dosing
- Demant et al. (2011) trained 44 Beagles on four schedules crossing frequency (once or twice a week against daily) with length (1 session against 3 in a row). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:136
- One session a week gave the best learning. Three back-to-back sessions every day gave the worst. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:138
- The schedule did not affect retention: every group kept the task well at four weeks. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:141
- The rule I give: fewer, cleaner, shorter sessions beat massed repetition, and spacing costs no retention. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:142
- For behaviour-change work, successful programmes used short, frequent sessions mixing habituation, relaxation and handler-dog communication. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:144

## Criteria rules for every plan
- Change one thing at a time: duration, distance or distraction. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:191
- Keep sessions short and spaced. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:193
- Do not raise criteria during the adolescent dip around eight months. Expect carer-specific cue failure and rebuild. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:194
- Never resolve a stall with a correction: confrontational techniques drew owner-directed aggression in up to 43% of surveyed cases. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:196
- If a trained behaviour suddenly falls apart in an adult dog, send the handler to a vet before changing the plan. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:198
- Keep an emotional record of what the dog finds unpleasant and what works. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:200
