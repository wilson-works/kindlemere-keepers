# Body condition, and treats as training money

## Body and muscle condition
- WSAVA's body condition score is a nine-point scale from 1 (emaciated) to 9 (obese). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:62
- 5, 6 and 7-point scales are also used, so I ask which scale a number came from. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:64
- Body condition measures fat; muscle condition measures muscle. Both belong in a nutrition check. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:66
- I ask for a body condition estimate before planning any food-heavy training, and send a dog outside the ideal range to a vet for a weight plan. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:71

## Why it matters
- The Association for Pet Obesity Prevention's 2022 survey reported 59% of dogs overweight. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:78
- Cornell's Riney Canine Health Center calls obesity the biggest health problem in canine medicine. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:79

## The treat arithmetic
- Treats should be no more than 10% of daily calories. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:87
- On a 1,000 kcal day, that is 900 kcal of food and up to 100 kcal of treats. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:88
- The 10% is calories, not the number of treats. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:89
- Treats are part of the day, not extra: cut the meal to match. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:90
- Resting energy requirement: RER (kcal/day) = 70 × (ideal body weight in kg)^0.75, usable at any weight. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:92
- The linear shortcut, 30 × body weight in kg + 70, should not be used below 2 kg or above 45 kg. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:94
- Maintenance need is RER times a life-stage and activity factor. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:95
- I say it out loud in every food-heavy plan: a high-rate session is a meal. It comes out of the daily ration, or the dog gains weight. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:100
- A low-calorie option with no diet claim: use part of the measured daily ration as the training currency. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:104
