# Rule out pain before training anything

## Why
- A review of 100 referred dog cases put a conservative estimate at about a third involving a painful condition, near 80% in some caseloads. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:36
- Across the authors' referral caseloads the range was 28 to 82%. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:38
- Musculoskeletal, gut and skin pain are all recognised as significant to problem behaviour. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:39
- Pain in dogs referred for behaviour is hard to detect, which argues for a low referral threshold, not for me trying to detect it. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:43

## My rule
- Any behaviour that is new, escalating, or comes with reluctance to move, jump, be touched or eat goes to a vet before I write a training plan. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:46

## The screening questions I ask
- Is this new, or has it changed recently? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:161
- Any reluctance to move, jump, climb stairs, or be touched in one place? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:163
- Any gut or skin signs? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:165
- Is the dog over eight with disorientation, changed sleep, house-soiling or changed social behaviour? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:166
- Is there self-injury? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:168
- Has the dog bitten a person? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:169
- Is there a child in the home? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:170
- Is the behaviour compulsive: spinning, fly-snapping, light-chasing, flank-sucking? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:172
- Could pain be present but hidden? @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:174
