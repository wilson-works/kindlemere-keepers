# Highly intelligent, high-drive dogs

## What the evidence supports
- Biddability was among the more heritable and more breed-differentiated traits, and more Border Collie ancestry tracked with more biddability. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:79
- Arden and Adams (2016) tested 68 farm Border Collies and found a general intelligence factor: the higher-order factor alone explained 17% of task-score variance, the full model 68%. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:81
- Read plainly: a dog that does well on one cognitive task tends to do well on others. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:84
- Chaser, a Border Collie, learned and kept the names of 1,022 objects over three years of intensive training (Pilley and Reid 2011). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:86
- Chaser also sorted common nouns into categories and inferred new names by exclusion, which needs concepts, not just word-object pairs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:88
- Earlier, Rico knew over 200 object labels and learned new ones after one exposure: "fast mapping" (Kaminski et al. 2004). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:90
- AKC calls the Border Collie "a remarkably bright workaholic ... maybe a bit too amazing for owners without the time, energy, or means to keep it occupied." @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:92

## What the evidence does not support
- A fixed hours-per-day exercise prescription per breed. Those numbers are trainer convention. Prescribe by observed outcome instead. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:95

## How to coach them
- Give the dog a trainable job with a clear start and finish. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:102
- Expect concept-level learning: raise criteria and add new discriminations rather than drilling mastered cues. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:104 @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:169
- Watch for compulsive repetition (staring, fly-snapping, light-chasing) in herding lines, and refer it rather than train through it. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:105
- Reward-based training improved later ability to learn a novel task (Rooney and Cowan 2011), which matters most when the dog's job is learning. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:107
