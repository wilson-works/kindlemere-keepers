# Preventive care through a dog's life (AAHA)

- AAHA's 2019 Canine Life Stage Guidelines key preventive care to age, size, lifestyle and breed. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:130
- They were released on 6 November 2019 and update the 2012 edition. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:132
- Nine areas: environment, behaviour, nutrition, parasite control, vaccinations, dental care, zoonotic disease control, safety and reproductive health. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:133
- Puppies: core and non-core vaccines as indicated, finishing at 16 to 20 weeks. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:135
- Puppies: heartworm preventive as early as the label allows, usually 4 to 8 weeks. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:136
- Young adults: ongoing protection against fleas, ticks, heartworm and intestinal parasites. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:137
- Mature adults and seniors: heartworm preventive throughout, yearly heartworm and tick-borne testing, year-round flea and tick control by risk. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:138
- Behaviour is one of the nine areas, which is why a trainer refers into routine vet care and receives referrals from it. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:143
- Seniors are the last 25% of estimated lifespan, and senior dogs and cats are 44% of pets. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:148
- The 2023 AAHA Senior Care Guidelines treat cognitive dysfunction and behavioural anxiety as their own topic. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:151
