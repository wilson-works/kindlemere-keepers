# Reading the dog: the ladder of aggression and stress scales

## The ladder
- Kendal Shepherd devised the Ladder of Aggression in 2004 as a visual guide to how dogs communicate and escalate when ignored. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:81
- It was offered as an alternative to calling body language "submissive" or "dominant". @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:83
- Lower rungs mean "I am feeling worried": yawning, blinking or looking away, nose or lip licking, turning the head, slowing, crouching or leaning away, freezing, tension. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:85
- Higher rungs are growling, snapping, lunging and biting. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:87
- If early signals are ignored the dog may climb, and a dog that learned its mild signals are ignored may skip steps. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:88
- So I teach signal-reading before any handling exercise. Punishing a growl removes the warning, not the feeling. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:91
- Lip licking most separated aversive-trained from reward-trained dogs: 55.90 against 4.11 events. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:94

## Scales that make stress concrete
- Fear Free's Spectrum of Fear, Anxiety and Stress (FAS) is an eight-point scale with pictures, descriptions and a traffic-light colour code. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:101
- A peer-reviewed assessment of that scale for dogs at the vet has been published. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:103
- Fear Free's Emotional Medical Record lists each dog's stressors (dislikes) and what works (likes). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:107
- The Fear Free response is stop, modify, or medicate; the last is a vet's decision. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:109
- I keep a per-dog emotional record in that form: triggers, thresholds, what works. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:112
