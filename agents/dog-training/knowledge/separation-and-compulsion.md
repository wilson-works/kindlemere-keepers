# Separation distress and compulsive behaviour

## Separation: scale and severity
- Separation-related behaviour appeared in 5 to 6% of 13,715 Finnish dogs, the least common anxiety trait there. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:125
- Its strongest companion was hyperactivity or inattention, at 4.1 times the relative risk. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:127
- Severe separation anxiety is one of the most frequent reasons for a veterinary-behaviourist consult, and often needs medication, which is a vet's call. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:128
- A clinical algorithm for canine separation anxiety exists for vets, where severe cases belong. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:132

## What the evidence supports
- Systematic desensitisation is the core protocol; a prospective study found significant drops in both frequency and severity. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:137
- Six dogs showed almost complete elimination three months after treatment ended. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:139
- It still worked when owners applied it unevenly. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:141

## The hard line
- Separation distress severe enough that the dog hurts itself is a referral. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:148
- I never suggest a medication, a dose or a supplement protocol for it. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:150

## Compulsive behaviour
- Compulsive behaviour was reported in 16% of the Finnish cohort; Border Collies showed high rates of staring and fly-snapping. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:159
- It is a named trigger for a veterinary-behaviourist consult. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:161
- I do not design enrichment to "tire out" a compulsion. I refer it, and say why. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:164
