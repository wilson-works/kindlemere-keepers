# What to ask about a dog at intake, and what is common

## Assess these per dog, in the handler's own words
- Reinforcer hierarchy: what food, toy, movement or access this dog will work for. Reinforcer value drives learning speed more reliably than breed. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:143
- Trainability or biddability: the one factor breed partly predicts. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:145
- Fear and non-social fear: the most common comorbidity, 29% of the Finnish cohort. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:147
- Noise sensitivity: the most common trait at 32%, and a frequent hidden driver of "stubbornness". @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:148
- Impulsivity: a documented risk factor for resource-guarding aggression. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:149
- Chase and predatory patterns: the behaviour most often called untrainable. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:150
- Attachment style: insecure attachment before adolescence predicted reduced obedience during puberty. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:152
- Pain status: about a third of behaviour referrals involve a painful condition, so pain is an intake item. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:154

## The instrument behind most of the data
- C-BARQ (Hsu and Serpell 2003, JAVMA) began as 68 items in 11 factors; the version in wide use has 78 items across 14 factors plus 22 standalone measures, online since 2005. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:48
- It measures fearfulness, aggression, separation-related behaviour, excitability, attachment, trainability, chasing and energy among others. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:52

## Base rates (Salonen et al. 2020: 13,715 Finnish pet dogs, 264 breeds)
- Noise sensitivity: 32% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:15
- General fearfulness: 29% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:16
- Fear of surfaces or heights: 23.5% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:17
- Inattention: 20% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:18
- Compulsive behaviour: 16% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:19
- Aggression: 14% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:20
- Separation-related behaviour: 5 to 6% @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:21

- Breed differences are real at the population level: 10.6% of Miniature Schnauzers were aggressive to strangers against 0.4% of Labradors. That describes distributions, not individuals. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:64
- Comorbidity changes the plan more than breed: separation-related behaviour with hyperactivity or inattention carried 4.1 times the relative risk, and fear with aggression 3.2 times. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:67
- So "reactive", "destructive" and "won't settle" are symptoms that cluster. Screen the whole cluster before fixing the presenting complaint. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:29
