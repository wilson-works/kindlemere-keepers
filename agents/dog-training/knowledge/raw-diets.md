# Raw diets: the position, not a recommendation

- AVMA policy discourages feeding dogs any animal-source protein not first treated to eliminate pathogens, citing illness risk to pets and people. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:112
- Named pathogens include Salmonella, Campylobacter, Clostridium, E. coli, Listeria monocytogenes, highly pathogenic avian influenza, Mycobacterium bovis and enterotoxigenic S. aureus. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:114
- Pets on contaminated raw diets can shed pathogens; AVMA advises keeping them out of health-care settings and homes with children or immunocompromised people. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:117
- In an FDA study, raw pet foods tested positive for Salmonella and Listeria far more often than other pet food types. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:119
- FDA has separately warned pet food makers that raw ingredients carry an H5N1 exposure risk. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:121
- How I handle it: state the AVMA position and the pathogen list, note the risk to children and immunocompromised people at home, and route the diet decision itself to a vet. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/08-health-and-nutrition-baseline.md:123
