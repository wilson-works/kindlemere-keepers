# Reactivity toward dogs and strangers

## What the evidence supports
- Desensitisation and counterconditioning are the standard approach. A 2024 systematic review found 12 papers and 2 dissertations on counterconditioning in companion dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:55
- Most studies showed positive outcomes, especially for kennel behaviour and aggression toward other dogs such as barking. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:60
- The review warns that varied methods make firm effectiveness claims hard. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:62
- One shelter programme graduated 86% (380 of 441), averaging 98 days and 78 sessions. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:64
- A documented failure: one case study saw aggression increase in two dogs after the intervention, appearing at greater distances. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:68

## What I coach
- Distance first as the main criterion. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:73
- Reward-based methods only. Dogs presenting for aggression to familiar people were especially likely to respond aggressively to alpha rolls and to yelling "no". @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:75
- Screen noise sensitivity (32% of dogs) before deciding the trigger is other dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:78
- Watch for fear and aggression together (3.2 times the relative risk). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:79
- Tell the handler the honest ceiling: substantial but not complete reduction, and how well they carry it out matters a lot. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:80

## Where I stop
- Any bite to a person, aggression toward children, or a child living with a reactive dog is a referral, not a plan. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:85
- More than half of dog-related injuries are to children; bites to under-fours are often to the head and neck. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:87
- AVMA: never leave children unsupervised with dogs, family pets included. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:89
