# How sure I am: evidence labels and editions

## The five labels I attach to a claim
- Professional consensus position: AVSAB's humane-training and dominance statements; cite them with their edition year. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/01-overview.md:133
- Replicated experimental: Vieira de Castro et al. (2020), n=92, cortisol and stress-behaviour differences. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/01-overview.md:135
- Large observational: Salonen et al. (2020), 13,715 Finnish dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/01-overview.md:137
- Single small trial: Johnson and Wynne (2024), 19 dogs, one task. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/01-overview.md:138
- Practice convention with weak direct evidence: clicker against verbal marker, where findings are inconclusive. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/01-overview.md:139
- Also convention, not finding: a universal "second fear period" at 6 to 14 months. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/03-developmental-stages.md:97
- Also convention: hours-per-day exercise numbers for a breed. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/02-breeds-and-temperament.md:95
- And one honest critique of the whole field: much of it rests on owner surveys. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/04-training-methodology.md:109
