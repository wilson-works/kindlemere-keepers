# The skills ladder, from foundations to the city test

## Why the AKC tests are the skeleton
- Most published progressions are trainer convention. The AKC Canine Good Citizen family is the main publicly specified, testable standard, so it is the skeleton and the rest is craft. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:11
- CGC has 10 items, all required, all on leash. No minimum age, but the dog needs the training and maturity to do all ten reliably. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:15
- Any dog may take it, purebred or mixed. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:18
- CGCA needs the CGC on record; CGCU is 10 items, all required, no age limit. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:21

## Tier 0: foundations (any age, start now)
- Build a reinforcer hierarchy first: the plan depends on what this dog will work for. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:31
- Teach attention ("look at me") first: it drew 0% owner-directed aggression in Herron's survey. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:33
- Teach a marker, and say a clicker is not proven better than a spoken word. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:35
- Hand-feeding and bowl work: adding food to the bowl and teaching "drop" went with less resource guarding; taking food away was a risk factor. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:37
- A start-button behaviour for cooperative care belongs here, not later. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:42

## Tier 1: the CGC ten
- CGC item 1: Accepting a friendly stranger, on leash. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:53
- CGC item 2: Sitting politely for petting; consent-based handling helps. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:54
- CGC item 3: Appearance and grooming; handling tolerance. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:55
- CGC item 4: Walking on a loose lead; a prong or choke collar is a red flag, not an option. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:56
- CGC item 5: Walking through a crowd; vary proximity, not duration. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:57
- CGC item 6: Sit and down on cue, staying in place; expect a dip near 8 months. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:58
- CGC item 7: Coming when called; reward-based beat e-collar training for recall in a 63-dog trial. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:59
- CGC item 8: Reaction to another dog; vary distance first. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:60
- CGC item 9: Reaction to distraction; screen noise sensitivity (32% of dogs) before blaming the dog. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:61
- CGC item 10: Supervised separation; severe separation distress is a referral, not a training item. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:62

- Most dogs are ready after a basic obedience course, in practice at least six months old. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:64

## Tier 2: CGCA, the same skills in the real world
- A controlled wait during paperwork; loose-lead turns, stops and pace changes; a real crowd; passing other dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:73
- A group sit-stay; a person with a bag approaching; "leave it" past food. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:77
- A down or sit on a 20-foot line while the owner fetches something; recall from 20 feet with distractions; a stay while the owner goes through a doorway. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:80
- The lesson: CGCA keeps the behaviours and changes only the context. It is a generalisation ladder. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:84

## Tier 2b: CGCU, city pressure
- Doorways without pulling; crowded pavements; horns, sirens, bikes, skateboards and changing surfaces. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:93
- Waiting at a corner then crossing; ignoring food on the ground; a stranger petting while carrying bags. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:96
- A 3-minute down-stay during the owner's meal; stairs and lifts; house-trained for city living; calm in cars or transit. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:99
