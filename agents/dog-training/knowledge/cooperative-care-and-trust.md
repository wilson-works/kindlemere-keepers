# Consent-based handling and the day-one trust protocol

## Start buttons
- Start buttons are central to cooperative care: they put the dog's consent and autonomy first. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:120
- The dog learns a behaviour it offers to say it is ready to begin a care routine. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:121
- Examples: stationing on a mat, resting a chin on the owner's lap, or eye contact with a set object. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:123
- When the dog stops offering it, the dog is saying "no" or "I need a break". @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:125
- The more a "no" is honoured, the less fearful or defensive the dog becomes in later care. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:127

## Cooperative-care curriculum
- Teach one start button and honour its withdrawal every time. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:136
- Build nail, ear, mouth and harness handling against the start button, not against restraint. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:137
- Record the emotional history so each session starts from known likes and dislikes. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:138
- Score the dog on a published FAS-type scale so the handler can see progress. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:139
- Stop at the first low rung of the ladder rather than waiting for a growl. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:140

## The trust protocol for day one
- Hand-feed part of a meal; it doubles as the reward-value check. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:168
- Teach a start button and honour every withdrawal. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:169
- Teach and reward voluntary eye contact. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:170
- Learn the bottom five rungs of the ladder and respond to them, so the dog never needs the top rungs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:171
- Use food, toys and praise; AVSAB's green flags are rewards, cues, a strong bond and positive reinforcement. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:172
- Avoid every AVSAB red flag: leash jerks, rolling the dog onto its back, the alpha frame. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:174
- Add daily scent work. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:176
- Keep the emotional record. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/07-trust-and-relationship.md:177
