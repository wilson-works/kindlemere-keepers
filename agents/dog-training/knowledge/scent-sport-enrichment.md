# Scent work, sport, and enrichment that has been measured

## Sport eligibility
- AKC agility requires the dog to be 15 months or older. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:143
- The dog must also be AKC-registered, PAL or ILP-listed, or registered through Canine Partners for mixed breeds. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:144

## Scent work, the best-evidenced lever
- Duranton and Horowitz (2019): 20 dogs aged 1 or older, 10 on nosework and 10 on heelwork, tested before and after two weeks of daily practice. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:173
- Nosework made dogs quicker to approach an ambiguous bowl: a more positive, "optimistic" judgement bias. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:175
- Scent in kennels cut stress behaviours and increased exploring. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:179
- Scented toys improved engagement and welfare signs in kennelled dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:181
- For a high-drive dog whose handler cannot add two hours of running, scent work is the evidence-backed substitute. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:158

## Chewing and foraging
- Chewing is linked to dental and oral hygiene, digestive health, bone strength, psychological health and stress management. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:187
- A food-stuffed latex cylinder plus daily reward sessions improved desirable behaviour in shelter dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:189
- Food enrichment increased foraging and activity in shelter dogs. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:194
- Treats used in training count toward a 10% daily-calorie cap. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:195

## The frame: Five Domains
- Experiences in nutrition, health, environment and behaviour feed the mental domain; more positive experiences mean better welfare. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:200

## A weekly prescription without invented numbers
- Daily scent or foraging work (measured benefit at two weeks). @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:225
- Training sessions spaced, not massed. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:226
- A chewing outlet. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:228
- A trainable job with a start and finish. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:229
- Measure success by the dog's behaviour at rest, not distance covered. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:231
- Training is enrichment, not an alternative to it: reward-based training improved later learning. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:218
- Honest strength note: "intelligent dogs get destructive when bored" is widely asserted; only the parts above are directly tested. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/06-behavior-and-enrichment.md:209
