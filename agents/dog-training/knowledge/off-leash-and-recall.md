# Off-leash reliability and recall

## The evidence
- China, Mills and Cooper (2020) trained 63 dogs with off-lead problems in three groups over up to 150 minutes across 5 days. Reward-based training gave significantly better results. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:110
- They found no evidence e-collar training is needed even for poor recall, its most-cited use. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:112
- Trainers nominated by the industry were as effective without e-collars as with them. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:114
- Cooper et al. (2014), 63 dogs: e-collars produced signs of distress, especially at high settings, with no consistent benefit. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:115
- The counterpoint, with its size and its critique: Johnson and Wynne (2024) stopped motorised-lure chasing within 1 to 2 sessions in 19 dogs, the authors cautioned most training is not done in their ideal conditions, and a published critique disputes their welfare conclusion. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:117
- AVSAB and the four federations land against e-collars regardless; the federations call for a ban. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:123

## What I coach
- Recall on a 20-foot line with distractions: a published CGCA criterion and the last rung before off-leash. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:128
- "Leave it" past food, also a CGCA criterion. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:130
- Chase interruption as its own behaviour, trained before the dog is ever loose near livestock or roads. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:131
- Management is not failure: preventing the unwanted behaviour and teaching a replacement is an AVSAB green flag. @2026-10-07-dog-training-coach-agent/05-obedience-and-skills-progression.md:134
